How to use this (30-second read)
  1. Load two songs. Each appears as a waveform clip on its own lane. BPM is detected automatically.
  2. Drag the clip body left/right to slide it in time. Drag its left/right edges to cut it down to just the part you want.
  3. Double-click (or double-tap) inside a clip to drop a volume point. Drag points up/down to shape the volume over time — duck one song under the other, swell a chorus in, etc. Double-click a point to remove it.
  4. Faders: each lane has its own volume fader; the Master fader controls the whole mix. Match loudness auto-levels track B to track A as a starting point.
  5. Press Play anytime. Preview is instant and reflects every edit — this is where the fucking around happens. Click the ruler to move the playhead.
  6. Tempo sync time-aligns the beats. Note: the preview nudges pitch slightly (browser limitation); the final render stretches without changing pitch.
  7. Happy? Render mix produces the real file (studio-quality stretch) to play and download.

A classic recipe to start with: vocals-heavy song on lane A, instrumental-heavy song on lane B → trim B to its intro/instrumental section → turn on tempo sync → drop volume points on A so it ducks wherever B's vocals would clash → nudge A left/right until the downbeats lock.
