SORRIO

A Mario-ish side scroller that plays itself — chiptune, combos, fireworks, and a bar graph judging its life choices.
Auto-Pilot: Sorrio runs, jumps, bonks ?-blocks and stomps on his own. On a phone: touch the left half of the screen for a joystick, tap the right half to jump — tap again mid-air to double jump, then hold to FLY. Keyboard: ← → and Space. Tap the screen after a run to restart. And whatever you do… don't press 😈.
